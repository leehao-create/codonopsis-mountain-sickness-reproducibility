# Figure 1. Network-pharmacology definition and functional context of the candidate-target set

**A**, Overlap between 398 database-derived putative *Codonopsis pilosula* targets (HERB and TCMSP) and 351 broadly retrieved mountain-sickness-associated genes (GeneCards and OMIM), yielding 54 shared candidates. Counts outside the intersection are shown within the respective sets. **B**, Archived STRING protein-protein interaction network containing 54 nodes and 543 interactions. All archived edges are displayed with low opacity to reduce visual crowding. Node size represents the frozen Cytoscape degree metric; the 15 highest-degree nodes are highlighted and the eight highest-degree nodes are labeled. Degree is interpreted as descriptive network connectivity, not evidence of therapeutic importance. **C**, Horizontal bar plot of the 15 highest frozen Cytoscape degree values. **D**, Five selected archived functional-enrichment terms. Horizontal position shows the archived `-Log(q-value)`, point size represents the number of genes in the corresponding archived display list, and color denotes the annotated collection. No network, degree or enrichment statistic was recalculated during figure redrawing.
